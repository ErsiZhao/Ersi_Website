About

I am a creative technologist, visual effects supervisor, and pipeline architect working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, visual computing, and cinematic storytelling.

My journey began in architectural engineering and design, where I developed a fascination with space, structure, and the relationship between environments and human experience. That foundation eventually led me into filmmaking and visual effects, where technology became a means of building worlds beyond physical reality.

I believe powerful stories have the ability to transport people beyond their everyday lives — to create a space to breathe, to feel, and to imagine. Throughout my career, my curiosity has been driven not only by what we can create, but by how we can create it differently.

Currently, I am a Senior Design Technologist at Amazon Prime Video, focusing on AI-driven content creation, emerging technologies, and the development of scalable creative workflows. My work brings together generative AI, computer vision, real-time rendering, and traditional production methodologies to explore new possibilities in visual storytelling.

Previously, as Head of Real-Time at Ingenuity Studios, I led multidisciplinary teams and developed real-time, virtual production, visual effects, and production automation pipelines across film, television, commercials, and music videos.

With a background spanning architecture, engineering, filmmaking, visual effects, and emerging AI technologies, I approach creative and technical challenges as interconnected problems rather than separate disciplines.

Ultimately, my goal is to build technologies that expand creative possibilities, reduce technical barriers, and empower artists and storytellers to focus on what matters most: the story.
